About ISWC 2025

Conversations
That Shape
Our Future

Since 2012, ISWC has been the dedicated platform for conversations on social protection, helping shape positive outcomes and policies for Malaysians. It connects the public, industry, academics, and policymakers with globally renowned experts.

This year's theme — "Living To A Hundred: Are We Prepared?" — initiates critical conversations on ageing and retirement, focusing on income security for the elderly, Malaysia's preparedness for an ageing population, mental wellbeing, and healthier lifestyles.

💼Retirement is not just a financial matter
🤝Belonging Leads to Health
🌍Everyone has a role to play
Main Topics

What We'll
Explore

01
Policies That Work
Evidence-based frameworks from around the world that have successfully supported ageing populations and what Malaysia can adopt.
02
Cool Innovations
Technology and social innovations transforming how we age.
03
Ageing In Place
Enabling older persons to live with dignity in their own homes and communities.
04
Housing for Older Persons
Age-inclusive housing design and urban planning for an older Malaysia.
05
Funding & Income Security
Pensions and sustainable income mechanisms to ensure financial dignity.
